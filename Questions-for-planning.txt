Questions for you
1. Is this Truman Library only, or Eisenhower and others too? This affects the project's size more than anything else.

3. Who would do the scanning, and on what equipment? Realist mounts probably won't fit a standard 35mm film scanner holder. Some commercial services also scan only one eye, which is how you end up with a 2008-175 situation.
4. Should depth products be in scope? I recommend deferring them until the pilot scans are in.
